About me

I am an AI researcher and machine learning engineer with a background in Computer Engineering and more than eight years of professional software engineering experience.

My research interests include Computer Vision, Medical AI, Deep Learning, Multimodal Learning, and Explainable AI. I am particularly interested in developing intelligent systems that can learn meaningful representations from complex visual and multimodal data.

My software engineering background allows me to bridge research and practical implementation, from developing machine learning algorithms to building production-oriented intelligent systems.
